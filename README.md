# 💥 Abu Madfa3 (Survivor.io Replica)
### *A tribute game to our dear Anthony*

A 2D top-down roguelite horde-survival auto-shooter inspired by **Survivor.io**, starring **Abu Madfa3** (*The Bazooka Survivor*). Battle tactical military swarms, level up, collect gear, evolve weapons into screen-clearing tactical nukes, and survive 4 epic boss battles across a grueling 20-minute mission!

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### Launching the Game
1. Simply double-click **`index.html`** in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Opera).
2. Runs 100% offline, zero installation or npm dependencies needed.

### Controls
* **Desktop (Keyboard)**:
  * `W` `A` `S` `D` or `Arrow Keys`: Move Abu Madfa3 in 8 directions.
  * `P` or `Escape`: Pause / Resume.
  * **Auto-Shoot**: The shoulder-mounted Bazooka automatically rotates to track incoming enemies and launches ballistic rockets with heavy explosive AoE splash!
* **Mobile / Touchscreen**:
  * Drag the virtual floating thumb joystick on the screen.

## 🏆 Colleague Leaderboard & Score Competition

Compete against your colleagues to see who can survive the longest and rack up the highest score!

### How Scores are Calculated:
$$\text{Score} = (\text{Survival Seconds} \times 20) + (\text{Kills} \times 35) + (\text{Level} \times 100) + (\text{Gold} \times 2)$$

### Features:
* **Record Your Run**: Enter your name or nickname on the Game Over or Victory screen to record your run into the hall of fame.
* **Top Ranks**: Displays medals (🥇, 🥈, 🥉) and ranks the top 20 scores stored locally in your browser.
* **📋 Copy / Export to Clipboard**: Click **COPY SCORES** in the leaderboard modal to generate a formatted text table that you can paste directly into Slack, WhatsApp, Teams, or email to challenge your coworkers!

## 🚀 Arsenal & Weapon Evolutions

* **RPG-7 Bazooka** (Levels 1–5): High Explosives, Twin Tubes, Napalm Ground Burn, and Cluster Bomblets.
* **EVO Evolution — ☢️ Doomsday Hydra Cannon**: Max Level 5 Bazooka + at least Level 1 **HE Fuel**. Fires salvos of tactical nukes with screen-wide chain explosions!
* **🪚 Spinning Blades (Guardian)**: Orbiting sawblades providing a defensive knockback zone.
* **⚡ Lightning Emitter**: Calls down vertical lightning bolts from the sky.
* **🛸 Attack Drones**: Autonomous drones hovering over your shoulders firing homing micro-missiles.
* **🍾 Molotov Cocktail**: Throws incendiary bottles that ignite persistent burning ground pools.
